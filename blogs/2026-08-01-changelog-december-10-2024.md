This is a removal that affects the App and Intranet. Starting December 10, 2024, Staffbase will discontinue support for the following generic URLs as reply URLs: de.eyo.net (DE) backend.staffbase.com (US) If you use any of these domains as reply URLs, you must update your Identity Provider (IdP) settings with your Staffbase platform domain to the list of allowed reply URLs.
