# Staffbase

> Staffbase is an AI-native employee experience and internal-communications platform — intranet, branded employee mobile app, email/newsletters, and an AI knowledge assistant — used by 1,500+ enterprises to reach frontline and distributed workforces. The Staffbase Platform API (HTTP Basic auth with an API token, base https://app.staffbase.com/api) covers users, groups, news, pages, media, notifications, spaces, campaigns, analytics and more; the Plugins/Custom-Widget SDKs embed apps and widgets inside the Staffbase experience.
